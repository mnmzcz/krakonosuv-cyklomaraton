# Surface brief: homepage (and site shell)

Scope: `src/pages/index.astro` plus the shared shell (header, footer) that every page inherits. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Related surfaces: `/fanousci/` (fans), all info subpages inherit this world.

Audience and job: amateur road cyclists deciding to ride and registering early; fans looking for where and when. Action: Registrace (SportSoft). Proof: real 2026 route profile, real split times, organisers' photography, testimonials, partner board. Constraints: the YouTube intro video stays as the hero; content of the old site stays verbatim.

Memorable moment: the real elevation profile of the race rises out of the bottom of the hero video and becomes the ground of the page; you can scrub along it km by km.

Build path: code-led (no image generation available in this session; the concept-seed launcher could not run, so the direction came from the structured question round, where the user picked it).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the race's road book. Like a Tour "profil de l'étape", the real elevation profile is the spine: places stand at their kilometre, peaks carry altitudes, everything else hangs off the road. Refuses the category default of full-bleed video, italic headline, countdown and a row of identical news cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ridge blue (deep #0D2440 fields, #1F4E86 mid, #8DB3DC haze) on cool snow white #F5F8FB; Casia red #E30613 only for the action and race markers. One grotesque in many widths (Archivo 62 to 100): extra-condensed caps for place labels and headlines, normal width for reading, tabular figures for km, m and Kč. Vertical place labels with leader lines, km rulers with ticks, profile fills shaded by gradient.

STORY: This is a real mountain road (2 489 m of climbing), others love it, registering now is cheapest, here is everything you need; fans: here is where and when.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Video full-bleed under a white header. Lower left: H1 in condensed caps, date line, two route facts, red Registrace plus price-now line. Bottom edge: the long-route profile silhouette in snow white, Pomezní Boudy and Pražská bouda labelled.

FORM: Road book / stage profile, the user's choice from four (ranked third on my list). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
